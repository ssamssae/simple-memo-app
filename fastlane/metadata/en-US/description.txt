Memoyo is a dark notepad for jotting things down instantly and keeping the notes you reread organized. You can record just the body with no title, so it fits short memos, ideas, to-dos, and daily notes like a diary.

■ Core features
· Title-free quick notes, stored locally on your device
· Swipe to favorite, drag to reorder
· Trash that lets you restore deleted notes
· Optional Google Drive backup and restore
· Use it right away, no account signup

■ Ads and removing ads
· The app is free and shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen.
· To remove ads, just make a one-time Remove Ads purchase inside the app.
· It is a single payment, with no recurring charges and no auto-renewal.
· You can restore the purchase after switching devices or reinstalling.

If you bought Remove Ads before, your entitlement stays intact.
